Privacy Policy

AceSAT Pro  |  Last updated: June 19, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how AceSAT Pro ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application ("App"). By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide:

  • Name (optional, used only for personalised greetings)
  • Target SAT score and test date (stored locally on your device)
  • Self-assessed skill levels during onboarding

Automatically collected information:

  • Practice session results, accuracy data, and study streaks — stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage
  • Subscription and purchase status via RevenueCat (see Section 4)
  • Standard app usage data collected by the Apple App Store or Google Play (subject to their respective privacy policies)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To personalise your study plan and surface weak topics
  • To calculate and display your estimated SAT score
  • To enforce daily question limits on the free tier
  • To process and verify your subscription
  • To improve the App (aggregated, anonymised analytics only)

3. Data Storage

Almost all data is stored locally on your device and never transmitted to our servers. Your name, scores, and study history do not leave your device unless explicitly described otherwise in this policy.

4. Third-Party Services

RevenueCat — We use RevenueCat to manage in-app subscriptions. RevenueCat may collect your device identifier, purchase receipts, and subscription status. Their privacy policy is available at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

Apple App Store / Google Play — Purchases and payments are processed by Apple or Google. We do not store your payment card information.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is designed for students aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

All locally stored data can be deleted at any time by going to Settings → Reset All Data within the App, or by uninstalling the App. To delete any RevenueCat-associated data, contact us at the address below.

7. Security

We use industry-standard practices to protect the information we hold. As most data is stored only on your device, the security of that data also depends on the security of your device.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
